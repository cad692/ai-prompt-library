Quick Cheat Sheet

Which tool lane?

  • Everyday writing / planning: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek (free-first)
  • Cited web research: Perplexity
  • Study your own PDFs: NotebookLM
  • Slides fast: Gamma or Canva
  • Images: Gemini / ChatGPT images; Midjourney for stylized art
  • Short video edit: CapCut
  • Office apps: Microsoft Copilot or Gemini in Workspace

Prompt recipe

Role + Task + Context + Format + Constraints + Examples. Replace every [PLACEHOLDER].

Quality checks

  • Did it invent sources?
  • Does it match your constraints?
  • Can you explain the answer in your own words?
