Prompt Engineering Foundations

A reusable way to get better answers from any major assistant.

The RTCFCE framework

  1. Role — who should the AI act as?
  2. Task — what exact job should it do?
  3. Context — audience, constraints, background.
  4. Format — bullets, table, email, slides outline.
  5. Constraints — length, tone, what to avoid.
  6. Examples — one short sample of the style you want.

Before / after

Weak: “Write about climate change.”

Stronger: “Act as a secondary-school science teacher. Explain climate change causes for 14-year-olds in Pakistan in 8 short bullets, then give 3 local everyday examples, and end with 3 quiz questions. Avoid jargon.”

Iterate

  • Ask for a critique of the first draft, then a revised version.
  • Say what to keep and what to change.
  • Request: “Ask me up to 5 clarifying questions before you answer.”

Reduce hallucinations

  • Tell it to label assumptions.
  • Ask for sources you can open, then open them yourself.
  • For study notes, prefer NotebookLM on your own uploaded files.
  • Never invent citations — require “I don’t know” when evidence is missing.
