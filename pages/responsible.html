Responsible AI Use

Use AI to learn faster — not to cheat or put private data at risk.

Academic integrity

  • Follow your university or exam board rules.
  • Use AI for planning, explaining, and feedback — not for submitting hidden AI writing as your own when that is banned.
  • Do not use prompts to bypass exam rules or AI-detection tools.
  • Cite sources you actually read.

Verify facts

  • Open links and check primary sources.
  • Treat medical, legal, and tax answers as starting points only.

Privacy — never paste

  • Passwords, OTPs, bank details, CNIC scans
  • Client secrets, unpublished grades, private student data
  • Anything you would not share on a public WhatsApp group

Disclosure

When a school, client, or publisher asks, say when AI helped. Honesty builds trust.
