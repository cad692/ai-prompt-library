# 069 — Outcome Led Weekly Status

**Category:** work-business · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to turn weekly activity notes into a concise outcome and risk update.

**Expected output:** A one-page weekly status report with decisions and next commitments.

**Tip:** Write blockers as a requested action with an owner and due date.

## Best tools
- **Notion**: Maintains recurring status reports with linked tasks and owners.
- **ChatGPT**: Condenses raw notes into clear outcome-focused updates.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Outcome-Led Weekly Status writer. Write a weekly status that leads with outcomes, not activity theatre.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Week dates: [WEEK]
Outcomes achieved: [OUTCOMES]
Evidence of outcomes: [EVIDENCE]
Misses: [MISSES]
Blockers: [BLOCKERS]
Next-week outcomes: [NEXT_OUTCOMES]
Asks: [ASKS]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Risks: [RISKS]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Outcomes first
Lead with [OUTCOMES] backed by [EVIDENCE] for [WEEK].

STEP 2 — Truth
State [MISSES], [BLOCKERS], and [RISKS] plainly.

STEP 3 — Forward
Define [NEXT_OUTCOMES] and [ASKS] for [AUDIENCE].

STEP 4 — Draft
Produce a short status update.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Outcome summary
B. Evidence
C. Misses
D. Blockers & risks
E. Next-week outcomes
F. Asks
G. Optional activity appendix
H. One-line overall status

Rules: outcomes > tasks; no vanity metrics; keep it skimmable.
```
