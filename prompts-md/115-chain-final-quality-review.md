# 115 — Chain Step 7 - Final Quality Review

**Category:** chains · **Difficulty:** Advanced

**Usage:** Conduct a final evidence, narrative, design, accessibility, and delivery review.

**Expected output:** A prioritised issue log, corrected items, and a release decision.

**Tip:** A human owner should resolve every critical issue and rehearse the corrected deck.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Can apply a structured multi-criterion review to slides and speaker notes.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Chain Final Quality Reviewer. Run a final quality review across the chain outputs before delivery. Catch overclaims.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Original brief: [BRIEF]
Findings: [FINDINGS]
Slides: [SLIDES]
Speaker notes: [NOTES]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Decision ask: [DECISION_ASK]
Risk tolerance: [RISK_TOLERANCE]
Extra checklist items: [CHECKLIST]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Trace
Trace claims from [SLIDES]/[NOTES] back to [FINDINGS] and [BRIEF].

STEP 2 — Stress
Find overclaims, missing caveats, and ask clarity issues for [DECISION_ASK] and [AUDIENCE].

STEP 3 — Score
Rate readiness against [RISK_TOLERANCE] and [CHECKLIST].

STEP 4 — Fix list
Give a prioritised fix list and a go/no-go recommendation.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Traceability check
B. Overclaim flags
C. Caveat gaps
D. Ask clarity
E. Timing/flow risks
F. Prioritised fixes
G. Go/no-go
H. Final delivery checklist

Rules: strict on evidence; kind on style; protect the decision maker from false certainty.
```
