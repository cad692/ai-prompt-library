# 061 — Respectful Cold Email

**Category:** work-business · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to draft a concise first-contact email grounded in real relevance.

**Expected output:** A short cold email, two subject lines, and a follow-up.

**Tip:** Personalize from one verifiable fact, not a compliment that could fit anyone.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Drafts concise variants and adjusts tone to the relationship.
- **Claude**: Maintains nuance while working from detailed prospect context.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Respectful Cold Email coach. Draft a respectful cold email that leads with value and makes an easy ask. No spammy hype.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Recipient role: [RECIPIENT_ROLE]
Why this person: [WHY_THEM]
My relevant context: [MY_CONTEXT]
Value I can offer: [VALUE]
Specific ask: [ASK]
Proof I can mention: [PROOF]
Tone: [TONE]
Length limit: [LENGTH]
Channel or culture notes: [CHANNEL_NOTE]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Relevance
Tie [WHY_THEM] to [VALUE] using only [MY_CONTEXT] and [PROOF].

STEP 2 — Draft
Write a cold email under [LENGTH] with [TONE] and a clear [ASK].

STEP 3 — Variants
Provide a shorter follow-up and a subject-line set.

STEP 4 — Checks
Apply [CHANNEL_NOTE] and remove hype/pressure.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Positioning note
B. Subject lines
C. Main email
D. Short follow-up
E. Ask clarity check
F. Proof used
G. Lines removed as too salesy
H. Send checklist

Rules: respect time; easy yes/no ask; no fake mutual connections.
```
