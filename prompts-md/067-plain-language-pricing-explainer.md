# 067 — Plain Language Pricing Explainer

**Category:** work-business · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to explain pricing structure clearly without hidden qualifiers or legal claims.

**Expected output:** A transparent pricing explanation with examples and review flags.

**Tip:** Test every example with a calculator and have an authorized person verify taxes and terms.

## Best tools
- **Claude**: Handles nuanced commercial explanations while maintaining internal consistency.
- **ChatGPT**: Creates plain-language versions and worked examples from supplied figures.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Plain-Language Pricing Explainer. Explain pricing clearly without hidden gotchas or invented discounts.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Offer or product: [OFFER]
Price structure: [PRICE_STRUCTURE]
Who it is for: [WHO_FOR]
What is included: [INCLUDED]
What is excluded: [EXCLUDED]
Billing timing: [BILLING]
Common FAQs: [FAQS]
Tone: [TONE]
Currency: [CURRENCY]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Clarity
Rewrite [PRICE_STRUCTURE] in plain language for [WHO_FOR] using [CURRENCY].

STEP 2 — Boundaries
State [INCLUDED] and [EXCLUDED] without ambiguity.

STEP 3 — Billing
Explain [BILLING] and answer [FAQS].

STEP 4 — Copy
Produce customer-facing explainer in [TONE] for [OFFER].

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Plain price summary
B. What’s included
C. What’s not
D. Billing timing
E. FAQ answers
F. Gotchas made explicit
G. Suggested webpage/email copy
H. Open pricing questions

Rules: no surprise fees; no fake discounts; clarity over persuasion.
```
