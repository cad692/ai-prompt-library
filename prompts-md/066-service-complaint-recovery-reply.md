# 066 — Service Complaint Recovery Reply

**Category:** work-business · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to respond calmly to a customer complaint with verified next steps.

**Expected output:** A concise customer reply with ownership, action, and follow-up timing.

**Tip:** Name the inconvenience specifically; generic empathy often sounds automated.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Adjusts tone and produces clear, customer-friendly service replies.
- **Gemini**: Summarizes supplied complaint details and drafts structured responses.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Service Complaint Recovery reply coach. Draft a recovery reply that owns the issue, protects dignity, and proposes a fair fix. No fake refunds.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Customer complaint: [COMPLAINT]
Facts I can confirm: [FACTS]
What went wrong: [WHAT_WENT_WRONG]
Customer impact: [CUSTOMER_IMPACT]
Fix options I can offer: [FIX_OPTIONS]
Limits I cannot exceed: [LIMITS]
Tone: [TONE]
Channel: [CHANNEL]
Follow-up promise I can keep: [FOLLOW_UP]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Acknowledge
Restate the problem using [FACTS] and [CUSTOMER_IMPACT] without blame-shifting.

STEP 2 — Own
Address [WHAT_WENT_WRONG] honestly within [LIMITS].

STEP 3 — Repair
Propose options from [FIX_OPTIONS] and a keepable [FOLLOW_UP].

STEP 4 — Draft
Write the [CHANNEL] reply in [TONE], plus an internal note.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Acknowledgement
B. Ownership statement
C. Repair options
D. Recommended reply
E. Internal note
F. Limits respected
G. Follow-up plan
H. Escalation trigger

Rules: no invented compensation; calm and specific; keep promises keepable.
```
