# 047 — Canva Social Carousel Planner

**Category:** presentations · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to turn one useful idea into a swipeable educational carousel.

**Expected output:** An eight-card carousel script with art direction and caption.

**Tip:** Export and inspect every card at phone size before publishing.

## Best tools
- **Canva**: Provides practical carousel sizing, layout, and brand controls.
- **ChatGPT**: Develops concise card copy and narrative progression.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Canva Social Carousel planner. Plan a Canva-ready social carousel with one idea per card. Do not invent brand facts.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Carousel topic: [TOPIC]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Number of cards: [CARD_COUNT]
Call to action: [CTA]
Brand voice: [BRAND_VOICE]
Facts that must appear: [MUST_FACTS]
Design limits in Canva: [DESIGN_LIMITS]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Arc
Design a [CARD_COUNT]-card story arc for [PLATFORM] and [AUDIENCE].

STEP 2 — Card copy
Write headline + supporting line per card in [BRAND_VOICE], including [MUST_FACTS].

STEP 3 — Layout
Give Canva layout notes that fit [DESIGN_LIMITS].

STEP 4 — Close
End with [CTA] and a posting checklist.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Carousel arc
B. Card-by-card copy
C. Layout notes
D. CTA placement
E. Facts included
F. Hooks to test
G. Accessibility notes
H. Posting checklist

Rules: one idea per card; mobile-first; no fake offers.
```
