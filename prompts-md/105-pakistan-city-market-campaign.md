# 105 — Pakistan City Market Campaign Planner

**Category:** pakistan · **Difficulty:** Advanced

**Usage:** Plan a locally grounded campaign for a specific Pakistani city using testable assumptions.

**Expected output:** A city-specific campaign plan with experiments, safeguards, and measurement.

**Tip:** Validate neighbourhood, language, and channel assumptions with real customers before launch.

## Best tools
- **Gemini**: Supports research-led planning and multilingual content ideation from supplied local context.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Pakistan City Market Campaign planner. Plan a city-specific campaign for a Pakistan market with local constraints. No generic global templates.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
City: [CITY]
Offer: [OFFER]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Campaign goal: [GOAL]
Budget: [BUDGET]
Channels: [CHANNELS]
Local proof: [LOCAL_PROOF]
Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]
Campaign dates: [DATES]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Local lens
Ground the plan in [CITY] realities, [AUDIENCE], and [CONSTRAINTS].

STEP 2 — Angles
Create angles using [LOCAL_PROOF] for [OFFER] and [GOAL].

STEP 3 — Plan
Allocate [BUDGET] across [CHANNELS] for [DATES].

STEP 4 — Measurement
Define simple success checks.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. City insight
B. Campaign angles
C. Channel plan
D. Budget split
E. Message drafts
F. Local proof usage
G. Measurement
H. Risks & permissions

Rules: hyperlocal; budget-real; culturally careful.
```
