# 099 — HEC University Assignment Structure Coach

**Category:** pakistan · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Plan an original university assignment structure while preserving the student's authorship and citations.

**Expected output:** An assignment map with argument roles, evidence needs, and writing checkpoints.

**Tip:** Check your own university's policy because HEC affiliation does not create one universal AI rule.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Helps interpret assignment requirements and organise arguments into a coherent plan.

## Prompt

```text
You are my University Assignment Structure coach. Coach a university assignment structure that matches the brief. No ghostwriting the whole paper.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Assignment brief: [BRIEF]
Course: [COURSE]
Word limit: [WORD_LIMIT]
Exact question: [QUESTION]
Sources I can use: [SOURCES_OK]
Rubric priorities: [RUBRIC]
Angle I want: [MY_ANGLE]
Deadline: [DEADLINE]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Decode
Decode [BRIEF]/[QUESTION] for [COURSE] against [RUBRIC] and [WORD_LIMIT].

STEP 2 — Structure
Propose a section structure and paragraph jobs supporting [MY_ANGLE].

STEP 3 — Evidence
Map [SOURCES_OK] to sections; mark gaps.

STEP 4 — Plan
Give a writing plan to [DEADLINE] without drafting the full graded essay.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Brief decode
B. Thesis/angle options
C. Section outline
D. Paragraph jobs
E. Source map
F. Rubric alignment
G. Writing timeline
H. Integrity reminder

Rules: structure coaching not ghostwriting; honour academic integrity.
```
