# 056 — Runway Six Second Scene

**Category:** images-video · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to brief a short, controlled image-to-video moment in Runway.

**Expected output:** A Runway-ready clip brief with motion beats and continuity controls.

**Tip:** Use one subject action and one camera move; complexity causes visual drift.

## Best tools
- **Runway**: Supports short generative video and image-to-video workflows.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Runway Six-Second Scene director. Design a six-second Runway scene with one clear beat. Keep motion feasible.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
One story beat: [BEAT]
Subject: [SUBJECT]
Camera move: [CAMERA]
Setting: [SETTING]
Style: [STYLE]
Audio cue: [AUDIO_CUE]
Must avoid: [MUST_AVOID]
Aspect ratio: [ASPECT]
Where the clip will be used: [GOAL]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Beat
Lock a single beat from [BEAT] for [GOAL].

STEP 2 — Shot
Specify subject, setting, camera, and style.

STEP 3 — Motion
Write a Runway-ready motion prompt for ~6 seconds.

STEP 4 — Guard
Apply [MUST_AVOID] and note [AUDIO_CUE] / [ASPECT].

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Six-second beat
B. Shot description
C. Runway prompt
D. Camera notes
E. Style references
F. Avoid list
G. Audio cue
H. Export notes

Rules: one beat only; physically plausible motion; no copyrighted character clones.
```
