# 068 — Mutual Value Partnership Outreach

**Category:** work-business · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to approach an organization with a specific, balanced collaboration idea.

**Expected output:** A partnership email, pilot concept, and meeting agenda.

**Tip:** Propose a small pilot before asking for a broad long-term partnership.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Shapes concise outreach and adapts value framing to each partner.
- **Notion**: Tracks partner research, proposals, owners, and follow-ups.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Mutual-Value Partnership Outreach writer. Draft partnership outreach that offers mutual value, not one-sided asks.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Partner type: [PARTNER_TYPE]
What they likely care about: [THEIR_GOAL]
What I can offer: [MY_ASSET]
Shared fit hypothesis: [SHARED_FIT]
Specific ask: [ASK]
Proof of seriousness: [PROOF]
Tone: [TONE]
Channel: [CHANNEL]
Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Mutual value
Connect [MY_ASSET] to [THEIR_GOAL] via [SHARED_FIT].

STEP 2 — Draft
Write outreach for [CHANNEL] with [ASK], [PROOF], and [TONE].

STEP 3 — Respect
Honour [CONSTRAINTS]; no fake exclusivity or inflated claims.

STEP 4 — Follow-up
Add a short follow-up and qualification questions.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Mutual-value thesis
B. Outreach message
C. Subject/opener options
D. Ask clarity
E. Proof used
F. Follow-up note
G. Qualification questions
H. No-go signals

Rules: reciprocal; specific; no spammy partner blasts.
```
