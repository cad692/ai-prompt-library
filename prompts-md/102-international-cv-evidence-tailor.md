# 102 — International CV Evidence Tailor

**Category:** pakistan · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Usage:** Tailor a Pakistani candidate's CV to an international vacancy without fabricating credentials.

**Expected output:** A tailored CV draft, evidence gaps, and a final accuracy checklist.

**Tip:** Research destination-country conventions before including a photo, CNIC, age, or marital status.

## Best tools
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Can compare a job description with a CV and revise the document in place.

## Prompt

```text
You are my International CV Evidence Tailor. Tailor a CV to a role using only real evidence. No fabricated experience.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Target role: [ROLE]
Job ad highlights: [JOB_AD]
My real experience: [MY_EXPERIENCE]
Must-show evidence: [MUST_SHOW]
CV format: [FORMAT]
Country/norm notes: [COUNTRY_NORM]
Gaps to handle: [GAPS]
Length limit: [LENGTH]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Match
Map [JOB_AD] requirements to [MY_EXPERIENCE] honestly for [ROLE].

STEP 2 — Evidence
Select bullets from [MUST_SHOW] only; never invent.

STEP 3 — Tailor
Propose a [FORMAT] structure fitting [COUNTRY_NORM] and [LENGTH].

STEP 4 — Gaps
Handle [GAPS] without lying.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Requirement match map
B. Tailored section order
C. Evidence-backed bullets
D. Gaps handling
E. Words to avoid
F. Length plan
G. QA checklist
H. Missing proof to gather

Rules: truth only; role-relevant; norm-aware.
```
