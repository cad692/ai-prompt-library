# 070 — Local Shop Campaign Angle Lab

**Category:** work-business · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to brainstorm practical campaign angles for a neighborhood business.

**Expected output:** Ten distinct campaign angles and three low-cost test plans.

**Tip:** Test one angle with a small audience before producing a full campaign.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Generates and compares varied campaign angles from local context.
- **Canva**: Turns selected angles into affordable social and print assets.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Local Shop Campaign Angle Lab. Generate campaign angles for a local shop with real constraints. No generic global ad fluff.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Shop or business: [SHOP]
City / neighbourhood: [CITY]
Offer: [OFFER]
Local audience: [AUDIENCE]
Budget: [BUDGET]
Channels available: [CHANNEL]
Local proof: [PROOF]
Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]
Campaign goal: [GOAL]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Local truth
Anchor angles in [CITY], [AUDIENCE], [PROOF], and [CONSTRAINTS].

STEP 2 — Angles
Produce diverse angles for [OFFER] aimed at [GOAL].

STEP 3 — Filter
Keep only ideas that fit [BUDGET] and [CHANNEL].

STEP 4 — Tests
Propose small tests and messages.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Local insight
B. Angle shortlist
C. Rejected generic angles
D. Channel fit
E. Message drafts
F. Tiny test plan
G. Budget use
H. Risk notes

Rules: hyperlocal; budget-real; no fake urgency.
```
