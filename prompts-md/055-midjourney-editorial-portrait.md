# 055 — Midjourney Editorial Portrait

**Category:** images-video · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use for a controlled editorial portrait with explicit lighting and camera language.

**Expected output:** A Midjourney-ready portrait prompt with variants and negative constraints.

**Tip:** Change one variable per reroll so you can identify what improved the portrait.

## Best tools
- **Midjourney**: Excels at stylized editorial portrait concepts and lighting treatments.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Midjourney Editorial Portrait director. Write a Midjourney-ready editorial portrait brief with ethical and likeness safeguards.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Subject type: [SUBJECT_TYPE]
Editorial story: [STORY]
Mood: [MOOD]
Wardrobe notes: [WARDROBE]
Setting: [SETTING]
Camera look: [CAMERA_LOOK]
Reference notes: [REF_NOTES]
Boundaries and bans: [BOUNDARIES]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Concept
Define the editorial concept from [STORY], [MOOD], and [SUBJECT_TYPE].

STEP 2 — Prompt
Write a Midjourney prompt with [WARDROBE], [SETTING], [CAMERA_LOOK], and [REF_NOTES].

STEP 3 — Ethics
Enforce [BOUNDARIES]: no real-person likeness cloning without rights; no harmful stereotypes.

STEP 4 — Variants
Give 3 prompt variants and negative cues.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Concept
B. Master Midjourney prompt
C. Variant prompts
D. Negatives / bans
E. Ethics notes
F. Upscale/crop tips
G. Caption idea
H. Revision knobs

Rules: editorial not creepy; respect consent and [BOUNDARIES].
```
