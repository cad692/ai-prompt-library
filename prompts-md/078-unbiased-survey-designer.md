# 078 — Unbiased Survey Designer

**Category:** data-productivity · **Difficulty:** Beginner

**Usage:** Use to design a short survey that measures one clear research objective.

**Expected output:** A field-ready survey instrument with rationale and pilot checks.

**Tip:** Pilot with five people and ask what they thought each question meant.

## Best tools
- **Google Forms**: Builds accessible surveys with common question types and branching.
- **Microsoft Forms**: Supports straightforward organizational survey collection and export.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Unbiased Survey designer. Design survey questions that reduce bias and collect usable answers.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Research goal: [RESEARCH_GOAL]
Respondents: [RESPONDENTS]
Must-learn topics: [MUST_LEARN]
Survey channel: [CHANNEL]
Target length: [LENGTH]
Sensitive topics: [SENSITIVE]
Language: [LANGUAGE]
Incentive notes: [INCENTIVE]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Goals
Map [RESEARCH_GOAL] and [MUST_LEARN] to constructs.

STEP 2 — Questions
Draft unbiased items for [RESPONDENTS] in [LANGUAGE], fit to [LENGTH] and [CHANNEL].

STEP 3 — Bias pass
Remove leading/double-barrelled items; handle [SENSITIVE] carefully.

STEP 4 — Fielding
Add intro, consent, and [INCENTIVE] notes plus analysis plan.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Construct map
B. Survey intro
C. Question list
D. Bias fixes made
E. Sensitive-item handling
F. Fielding plan
G. Analysis plan
H. Limitations

Rules: neutral wording; short; ethical; no fake population claims.
```
