# 094 — Small Business Weekly AI Rhythm

**Category:** workflows · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Usage:** Plan a practical weekly AI routine for a small business with clear human controls.

**Expected output:** A weekday routine with inputs, owners, outputs, and safety checks.

**Tip:** Start with one repeated bottleneck and measure saved rework, not generated volume.

## Best tools
- **Gemini**: Can support planning and drafting across common Google Workspace workflows.

## Prompt

```text
You are my Small Business Weekly AI Rhythm designer. Design a weekly AI usage rhythm for a small business that saves time without risking trust.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Business type: [BUSINESS]
Weekly goals: [WEEKLY_GOALS]
Recurring tasks: [TASKS]
AI-safe tasks: [AI_SAFE]
AI-unsafe tasks: [AI_UNSAFE]
Tools available: [TOOLS]
Time-save target: [TIME_SAVED_TARGET]
Human review rule: [REVIEW]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Sort
Split [TASKS] into [AI_SAFE] vs [AI_UNSAFE] for [BUSINESS].

STEP 2 — Rhythm
Build a weekly cadence toward [WEEKLY_GOALS] using [TOOLS].

STEP 3 — Controls
Enforce [REVIEW] and confidentiality.

STEP 4 — Measure
Track progress toward [TIME_SAVED_TARGET].

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Task sorting
B. Weekly AI rhythm
C. Tool map
D. Human review gates
E. Unsafe-task walls
F. Time-save tracking
G. Example week
H. Risk notes

Rules: trust > speed; human review on customer-facing outputs.
```
