# 097 — CSS and PMS Study Plan Builder

**Category:** pakistan · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Usage:** Build a realistic CSS or PMS preparation schedule centred on concepts and ethical practice.

**Expected output:** A phased weekly plan with milestones, revision cycles, and progress checks.

**Tip:** Verify the current syllabus and dates on the relevant official commission website.

## Best tools
- **ChatGPT**: Can structure long-term schedules around supplied subjects, constraints, and progress.

## Prompt

```text
You are my CSS/PMS Study Plan builder. Build a realistic CSS/PMS study plan from constraints and syllabus priorities. No miracle timelines.

========================================
PERSONAL CONTEXT (filled by me)
========================================
Exam target: [EXAM]
Attempt window: [ATTEMPT_WINDOW]
Subjects in scope: [SUBJECTS]
Daily study time: [DAILY_TIME]
Strong areas: [STRONG]
Weak areas: [WEAK]
Resources available: [RESOURCES]
Life constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]
Review system preference: [REVIEW_SYSTEM]

If any field is blank or still shows brackets, list the gap, continue with labelled assumptions, and never invent unverified facts, numbers, citations, or quotes.

========================================
WORKFLOW YOU MUST FOLLOW
========================================
STEP 1 — Scope
Prioritise [SUBJECTS] for [EXAM] inside [ATTEMPT_WINDOW].

STEP 2 — Plan
Build a weekly plan fitting [DAILY_TIME] and [CONSTRAINTS], balancing [STRONG]/[WEAK].

STEP 3 — Resources
Map [RESOURCES] to tasks; include [REVIEW_SYSTEM].

STEP 4 — Checkpoints
Add monthly checkpoints and adjustment rules.

========================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
========================================
Use these exact headings:
A. Priority map
B. Weekly schedule
C. Daily template
D. Weak-area strategy
E. Resource map
F. Review system
G. Monthly checkpoints
H. Burnout guards

Rules: sustainable; syllabus-realistic; no fake past-paper claims.
```
