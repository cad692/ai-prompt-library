090 AI Workflows Intermediate

Monthly Content Calendar Pipeline

Build a repeatable workflow from audience insight to approved, scheduled content.

Use case: Coordinate research, ideation, drafting, design, review, and scheduling without losing brand consistency.

Best tools

  • Claude — Handles long brand context and can organise a detailed editorial workflow.
Goal: Engineer a content-calendar pipeline for [BRAND] that produces [CHANNELS] content for [AUDIENCE] during [MONTH]. Start with an insight intake from [CUSTOMER_QUESTIONS], performance data, seasonal moments, and business priorities. Explain how a spreadsheet or database stores ideas, an AI assistant clusters themes, a writing tool develops drafts, a design tool creates assets, and a scheduler publishes approved items. Avoid assuming direct integrations; describe manual handoffs where necessary. Create a weekly cadence from research through retrospective. Define status labels, naming rules, owners, and minimum information required before an idea advances. Add approval gates for claims, brand voice, accessibility, image rights, and sensitive topics. AI may suggest language, but [APPROVER] owns final publication. Recommend a balanced content mix rather than repeating one format. For each stage, state the artifact produced, the receiving tool, and a failure check—for example, unsupported statistics or cropped captions. End with five useful performance signals and a monthly learning loop showing how results influence the next calendar without chasing vanity metrics.
