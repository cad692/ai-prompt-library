Chain Step 1 - Research Brief

Define a precise research brief that can support the rest of a presentation workflow.

Use case: Establish scope, audience, questions, and evidence standards before collecting sources.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can turn an initial request into a structured and testable research brief.
Role: You are the research lead beginning a seven-step presentation chain; produce a decision-ready brief for [TOPIC]. Review any earlier context in [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT]. Because this is step one, it may contain a rough request, stakeholder note, or “none.” Treat it as input to clarify, not as completed research. Define the intended audience [AUDIENCE], decision or learning goal [GOAL], delivery date [DEADLINE], and constraints [CONSTRAINTS]. Write a brief containing: background, central research question, five subordinate questions, in-scope and out-of-scope boundaries, required geographies and dates, key definitions, stakeholder perspectives, acceptable source types, and evidence quality rules. List assumptions separately and turn important unknowns into clarification questions. Specify the downstream artifacts: evidence notes, synthesis, outline, slide draft, speaker notes, and final review. Add completion criteria so the next step can judge whether research coverage is adequate. Do not research the topic yet or fabricate sources. End with a clearly labelled “HANDOFF TO STEP 2” block containing the approved brief and a compact search plan that can be pasted as [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT].
