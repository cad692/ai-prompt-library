Chain Step 2 - Evidence Notes

Convert the approved research brief into traceable, source-level evidence notes.

Use case: Collect relevant claims and limitations before interpretation begins.

Best tools

  • Perplexity — Supports source discovery with links that can be checked against originals.

Fill in your details

Your filled prompt (preview)

Goal: Act as the evidence analyst for step two; complete evidence notes from the research brief in [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT]. If that input lacks scope, audience, research questions, or source standards, state what is missing and reconstruct a provisional brief from [TOPIC] before proceeding. For each supplied or discovered source [SOURCES], record author or organisation, title, date, URL or document identifier, source type, and access date. Build notes around the brief's subordinate questions. Each note must contain one claim, a short exact quotation or precise location, relevant figures with units and period, the source's method or basis, limitations, and a relevance label. Distinguish primary evidence, analysis, opinion, and promotional content. Record contradictory findings side by side instead of forcing agreement. Do not invent citations or imply that an inaccessible source was read. Mark weak coverage, stale evidence, and claims needing corroboration. Include a coverage matrix linking questions to sources. Finish with “HANDOFF TO STEP 3”: a clean evidence ledger, unresolved gaps, and a list of claims safe—or unsafe—to synthesise. This handoff must stand alone when reused as [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT].
