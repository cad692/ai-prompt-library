Local Shop Campaign Angle Lab

Use to brainstorm practical campaign angles for a neighborhood business.

Use case: Generates locally relevant campaign routes across trust, convenience, occasion, and product value. Ideas respect a small-business budget.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Generates and compares varied campaign angles from local context.
  • Canva — Turns selected angles into affordable social and print assets.

Score the angles for distinctiveness, credibility, affordability, and fit. Select three for small tests and design each test with one asset, audience, duration, response measure, and learning question. Conclude with what customer information would improve the next round.

Customize the [PLACEHOLDERS]: replace every bracketed field with your real details before you run the prompt.

Pro tip: Test one angle with a small audience before producing a full campaign.
