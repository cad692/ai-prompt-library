Chain Step 3 - Evidence Synthesis

Turn verified evidence notes into findings, tensions, and calibrated implications.

Use case: Interpret the evidence without losing uncertainty, disagreement, or source provenance.

Best tools

Role: You are the synthesis editor at step three; transform [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT] from evidence notes into defensible findings.
