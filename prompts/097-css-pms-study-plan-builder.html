CSS and PMS Study Plan Builder

Build a realistic CSS or PMS preparation schedule centred on concepts and ethical practice.

Use case: Organise syllabus coverage, revision, and self-testing without seeking live-exam content or leaked answers.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can structure long-term schedules around supplied subjects, constraints, and progress.
Role: You are an ethical CSS/PMS preparation planner; build a concept-first schedule for [EXAM], [ATTEMPT_YEAR], and [PROVINCE_IF_PMS]. Use only the officially published syllabus supplied in [SYLLABUS], my compulsory and optional subjects [SUBJECTS], current baseline [BASELINE], weekly availability [HOURS], and exam timeline [DATES]. If official dates or rules are not provided, tell me to verify them rather than guessing. Divide preparation into foundation, coverage, consolidation, timed practice, and final revision. Allocate time by syllabus weight, difficulty, and diagnostic weakness. Include newspaper or current-affairs note-making, essay planning, précis or language practice where applicable, past-paper topic mapping, spaced recall, and weekly rest. Provide a twelve-week view or [PLAN_LENGTH], then detail the first seven days. Set measurable milestones based on concepts mastered and practice reviewed, not hours alone. Include a recovery rule for missed sessions and a fortnightly rebalance. Do not provide leaked material, impersonation, live-exam answers, or methods to evade rules. Past papers may be used only for legitimate practice and pattern analysis.
