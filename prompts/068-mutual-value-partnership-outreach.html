Mutual Value Partnership Outreach

Use to approach an organization with a specific, balanced collaboration idea.

Use case: Builds a partnership message around credible overlap and mutual contribution. It avoids vague requests to collaborate.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Shapes concise outreach and adapts value framing to each partner.
  • Notion — Tracks partner research, proposals, owners, and follow-ups.
