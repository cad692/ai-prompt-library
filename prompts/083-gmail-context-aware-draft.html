Gmail Context-Aware Draft

Draft a clear Gmail reply grounded in the thread and the sender's actual request.

Use case: Use Gemini in Gmail to prepare a reply while keeping commitments and sensitive details under human control.

Best tools

  • Gemini in Gmail — Uses the open email thread as context for drafting and summarising.
