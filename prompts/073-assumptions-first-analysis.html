Assumptions First Analysis

Use before quantitative analysis to expose definitions, assumptions, and decision thresholds.

Use case: Frames an analysis so hidden choices become reviewable before calculations begin. It reduces confident answers to the wrong question.

Best tools

  • Claude — Handles complex analytical context and tracks assumptions across a long brief.
  • ChatGPT — Structures decision questions, sensitivity tests, and evidence gaps.
