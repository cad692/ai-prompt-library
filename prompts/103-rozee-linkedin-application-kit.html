103 Pakistan Pack Beginner

Rozee.pk and LinkedIn Application Kit

Prepare a consistent application kit for a genuine vacancy found on Rozee.pk or LinkedIn.

Use case: Create tailored materials and follow-up messages while screening for suspicious listings.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can compare vacancy details with candidate evidence and draft coordinated materials.
Role: Act as my Pakistan job-application desk; assemble an honest kit for [VACANCY_TEXT] found on [ROZEE_OR_LINKEDIN]. Compare the vacancy with [MY_CV] and [CAREER_GOALS]. First produce a fit verdict—apply, clarify, or skip—with evidence. Note missing salary, location, schedule, contract type, and application deadline. Flag scam indicators such as payment requests, personal email domains, pressure to share banking details, or unverifiable company identity, while acknowledging that a flag is not proof. For a suitable role, prepare: a targeted three-line profile, six prioritised CV bullet selections or rewrites, a brief platform application message, and a respectful networking note to [CONTACT_TYPE]. Keep every claim supported by my materials and avoid generic enthusiasm. Add a tracker row with job title, company, source URL, date found, deadline, documents used, status, and follow-up date. Include five questions to investigate before interview. Do not submit anything, contact anyone, or disclose CNIC, bank details, passwords, or references without my explicit decision.
