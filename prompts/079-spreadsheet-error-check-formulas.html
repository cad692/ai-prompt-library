Spreadsheet Error Check Formulas

Use to design formula-based checks that flag likely spreadsheet errors.

Use case: Suggests review formulas for Excel or Google Sheets without overwriting source values. It emphasizes explainable flags and testing.

Best tools

  • Excel/Sheets — Supports transparent formulas, validation rules, and conditional formatting.
  • ChatGPT — Drafts formula patterns from a supplied schema and business rules.
