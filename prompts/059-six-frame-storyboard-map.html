059 Images & Video Beginner

Six Frame Storyboard Map

Use to previsualize a short video before filming or generating assets.

Use case: Maps a complete micro-story into six visually distinct frames. Each frame connects composition, camera, lighting, action, and sound.

Best tools

  • Canva — Makes storyboard frames and annotations easy to arrange.
  • Adobe Firefly — Generates consistent visual references for individual frames.

Take the role of a storyboard artist compressing [STORY_OR_MESSAGE] into exactly six purposeful frames. For each frame, provide a thumbnail description, composition and subject placement, shot size, camera angle, lens feel, camera or subject motion leading into the next shot, lighting direction and continuity, dominant colour, visible action, narration/dialogue, ambient sound, and approximate duration. Transitions should arise from matched action, gaze, sound, or graphic shape rather than random effects.
