Thesis Chapter Architecture Planner

Design a thesis chapter map aligned with the research question, method, and evidence.

Use case: Help a postgraduate researcher organise chapters and argument flow before drafting.

Best tools

  • Claude — Handles long research context and can compare argument structure across chapters.
Goal: Serve as a thesis architecture adviser and map chapters for [DEGREE_PROGRAMME] research on [TOPIC]. Work from my research question [QUESTION], objectives [OBJECTIVES], methodology [METHOD], available evidence [EVIDENCE], theoretical frame [FRAMEWORK], and departmental requirements [GUIDELINES]. Do not assume every thesis needs the same chapter pattern. Offer up to two viable architectures and explain when each works. For the chosen structure, define each chapter's job, central claim or contribution, inputs, planned sections, connection to the previous chapter, and evidence still needed. Show where literature is reviewed, method is justified, findings are presented, and interpretation occurs without duplicating material. Create an argument thread that can be followed from problem to contribution. Identify dependencies—for example, analysis that cannot begin before coding—and likely scope creep. Add chapter-specific questions for a supervisor meeting and completion criteria that are stronger than word count. Do not fabricate citations, results, participants, approvals, or quotations. Mark ethical and institutional decisions for confirmation with the supervisor or relevant review body.
