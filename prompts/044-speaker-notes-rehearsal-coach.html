Speaker Notes Rehearsal Coach

Use to convert slide bullets into natural notes for a timed presentation.

Use case: Produces spoken notes that complement rather than read the screen. It also builds timing and recovery cues for rehearsal.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Turns slide context into conversational, audience-aware speaking notes.
  • PowerPoint+Copilot — Keeps notes close to the slides used during delivery.
