104 Pakistan Pack Intermediate

Upwork and Fiverr Proposal Proof Writer

Draft a concise freelance proposal grounded in the client's brief and the seller's real proof.

Use case: Help Pakistani freelancers respond specifically without false portfolios, fake urgency, or platform-rule violations.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can connect project requirements to supplied work samples and formulate clarifying questions.
Goal: Write an evidence-based proposal for [PLATFORM] opportunity [CLIENT_BRIEF] using my actual profile [FREELANCER_PROFILE]. Diagnose the client's likely outcome, deliverables, constraints, and unanswered questions. Identify up to three genuine proof points from [WORK_SAMPLES] that are relevant; if none fit, say so rather than inventing a portfolio item. Open with a specific observation about the work, not a generic greeting or claim to be “the best.” Draft a proposal of [LENGTH] that explains my approach, a sensible first milestone, communication rhythm, and what I need from the client. Where scope is uncertain, give conditional options instead of a fixed promise. Include three concise questions that expose important assumptions. Add a private review section checking feasibility, timeline, ownership of source files, confidentiality, platform communication rules, and suspicious requests. Do not suggest off-platform evasion, copied samples, false testimonials, unpaid speculative work beyond a reasonable boundary, or guarantees outside my control. End with two alternative closing lines: one direct and one warm. Preserve my natural voice [VOICE_SAMPLE].
