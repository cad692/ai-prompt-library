Chain Step 4 - Presentation Outline

Shape synthesised findings into a persuasive and logically paced presentation outline.

Use case: Decide the narrative sequence and slide jobs before writing slide content.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can organise findings into audience-focused narrative structures and transitions.
Goal: Serve as the presentation architect for step four; build an outline from the synthesis in [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT]. Confirm the audience [AUDIENCE], desired outcome [OUTCOME], time available [DURATION], and delivery setting [SETTING]. If these are absent, state reasonable provisional assumptions. Choose a narrative shape—problem/response, question/answer, chronological, comparison, or another justified structure—based on the findings rather than habit. Propose [SLIDE_COUNT] slides. For each, specify its single purpose, headline takeaway, evidence IDs to use, suitable visual form, and transition to the next idea. Include an opening that earns attention without sensationalism, a short method or evidence note, space for contradictions, and a close aligned with the requested outcome. Check pacing against the duration and remove redundant slides. Do not draft full slide copy, design decoration, or speaker notes yet. Flag any finding that cannot be simplified without distortion. End with “HANDOFF TO STEP 5”: the numbered slide architecture, evidence-to-slide map, visual cautions, and exact narrative through-line.
