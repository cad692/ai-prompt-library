Before and After Slide Clinic

Use to diagnose and redesign one weak slide without changing its facts.

Use case: Critiques a slide against audience needs and proposes a concrete after-state. The method separates message problems from visual problems.

Best tools

  • Canva — Makes the recommended layout changes easy to prototype.
  • PowerPoint+Copilot — Helps revise content inside a common business presentation workflow.
