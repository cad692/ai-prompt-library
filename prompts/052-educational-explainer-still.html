Educational Explainer Still

Use to visualize a process or concept in one teachable image.

Use case: Creates a labelled or label-ready educational still with a clear reading order. It avoids decoration that competes with learning.

Best tools

  • Adobe Firefly — Produces custom explanatory illustrations in controlled styles.
  • Canva — Adds accurate labels and accessible layout after image generation.
