CSS and PMS Concept Self-Tutor

Learn a CSS or PMS concept through explanation, examples, recall, and self-testing.

Use case: Build durable understanding of a syllabus topic without outsourcing assessed work.

Best tools

  • Claude — Can explain nuanced concepts and generate structured self-tests from supplied sources.
Goal: Teach me [CONCEPT] from the [CSS_OR_PMS_SUBJECT] syllabus until I can explain and apply it independently. Start by asking for my current understanding in two or three sentences. Then give a layered explanation: a plain-language definition, the key mechanism or argument, its Pakistan relevance where genuinely applicable, one concrete example, and one common misconception. Base the lesson on [SOURCES_OR_NOTES]; label anything that requires current verification. Use a teach-back pause before testing me. Create six self-test items in mixed forms: recall, distinction, application, evidence evaluation, counterargument, and a short outline. Do not answer them immediately. Wait for [MY_ANSWERS], then assess each against explicit criteria, identify the misconception behind any error, and offer a targeted retry question. Finish with a compact revision card containing five key terms, three relationships, and two credible source types to consult. This is study support only: do not answer an active examination, reproduce restricted material, or imply guaranteed marks.
