001 AI Assistants Intermediate

Decision Lens Builder

Use this to compare important options without outsourcing the final decision.

Use case: A student choosing a degree or a professional weighing job offers can expose trade-offs, assumptions, and low-risk tests.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Supports an interactive comparison and can reorganize changing priorities quickly.
  • Claude — Handles nuanced personal context and long option descriptions well.

Fill in your details

We ask only for the parts in square brackets. Your answers are inserted into the prompt below.

Your filled prompt (preview)

Act as a decision-framing partner whose goal is to clarify my choice, not make it for me. My decision is [DECISION]. The options are [OPTIONS]. My deadline is [DEADLINE], and the people affected are [STAKEHOLDERS]. What matters to me includes [VALUES_OR_PRIORITIES]. My practical limits are [TIME_MONEY_LOCATION_OR_FAMILY_CONSTRAINTS]. Begin by restating the decision neutrally. Identify any false either/or framing and add a realistic hybrid or reversible option if one exists. Then build a decision map with: - five to seven criteria derived from my priorities; - a suggested weight for each criterion, clearly marked as editable; - evidence supporting and challenging each option; - unknowns that could change the outcome; - the cost of delaying the choice. Do not pretend subjective scores are facts. Show a concise, visible rationale for each proposed weight and rating. Separate facts I supplied from your assumptions. Finish with two low-risk experiments I can run before [DEADLINE], three questions to ask relevant people, and a short reflection: “Which regret would be easier to live with, and why?” End by reminding me that I retain the decision.
