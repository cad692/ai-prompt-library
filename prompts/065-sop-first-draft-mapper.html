SOP First Draft Mapper

Use to turn process knowledge into a testable first SOP.

Use case: Documents a repeatable workflow with controls, exceptions, and ownership. The draft is designed for validation by the people doing the work.

Best tools

  • Notion — Publishes searchable SOPs with linked templates and revision history.
  • Claude — Structures detailed process notes and edge cases coherently.
