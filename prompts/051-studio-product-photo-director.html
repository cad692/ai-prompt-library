Studio Product Photo Director

Use to create a production-ready product image prompt without false features.

Use case: Directs an AI product visual for e-commerce or campaigns. The prompt preserves product identity and specifies a controlled studio setup.

Best tools

  • Adobe Firefly — Creates controlled commercial-style imagery with detailed visual direction.
  • Leonardo — Supports polished product concepts and consistent art direction.
