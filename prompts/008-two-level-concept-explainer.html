Two-Level Concept Explainer

Use this to understand a concept first simply and then at professional depth.

Use case: A learner can build intuition before encountering technical vocabulary, equations, caveats, or domain-specific applications.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Adapts explanations to different knowledge levels and supports follow-up questions.
  • Gemini — Explains concepts using varied examples and can work with supplied visuals.

Teach me [CONCEPT] twice: first as if I am 15, then as if I am entering the profession.

My current knowledge is [WHAT_I_ALREADY_KNOW]. I need this for [PURPOSE], in the field of [FIELD]. Use examples relevant to [LOCAL_OR_PERSONAL_CONTEXT].

Part One — Build Intuition
Explain the idea in plain language with one everyday analogy and a small concrete example. Define no more than three essential terms. Include one common misunderstanding and a quick “check yourself” question. Avoid saying something is “just simple” or “obvious.”

Part Two — Add Expert Precision
Revisit the same example using correct terminology, mechanisms, assumptions, and limitations. If equations matter, define every symbol and walk through a tiny numerical example. Distinguish what experts generally agree on from what depends on context.

The Bridge
Map each element of the first analogy to the technical explanation and state where the analogy breaks down. Show three visible reasoning steps for applying the concept to [APPLICATION], without claiming access to hidden internal reasoning.

End with a five-item ladder: what to learn next, from easiest to hardest. Ask me one transfer question that requires applying the concept to a new situation; do not answer it until I try.
Pro tip: After reading both levels, explain the connection between them in your own words.
Expected output: A teen-friendly explanation, expert treatment, and bridge between the two.
