Brand Consistent Image Family

Use to generate several campaign images that feel related but not repetitive.

Use case: Defines a reusable visual grammar for a coordinated image set. It separates fixed brand rules from scene-level variation.

Best tools

  • Leonardo — Supports consistent visual development across a coordinated image series.
  • Adobe Firefly — Generates varied campaign scenes from controlled brand art direction.
