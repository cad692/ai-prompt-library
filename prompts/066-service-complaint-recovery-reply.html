Service Complaint Recovery Reply

Use to respond calmly to a customer complaint with verified next steps.

Use case: Drafts an empathetic complaint response without admitting unverified facts or making unauthorized promises. It centers resolution and ownership.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Adjusts tone and produces clear, customer-friendly service replies.
  • Gemini — Summarizes supplied complaint details and drafts structured responses.
