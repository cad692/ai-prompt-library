Focused Job-Hunt Daily System

Organise a daily job search across discovery, tailoring, outreach, tracking, and follow-up.

Use case: Create a sustainable routine that prioritises suitable applications over indiscriminate volume.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can coordinate tailoring, rehearsal, planning, and reflection from supplied information.

Fill in your details

We ask only for the parts in square brackets. Your answers are inserted into the prompt below.

Your filled prompt (preview)

Role: Be my job-search operations coach; create a daily workflow for [TARGET_ROLES] in [LOCATION_OR_REMOTE_PREFERENCE]. I have [DAILY_TIME] available and use [JOB_BOARDS], [DOCUMENT_TOOL], [AI_ASSISTANT], and [TRACKER]. Divide the session into opportunity discovery, fit screening, CV tailoring, cover note preparation, networking, application submission, and follow-up. Give each block a realistic time limit and a definition of done. Build a fit score using essential skills, evidence from my background [CANDIDATE_PROFILE], location, work arrangement, and genuine interest. Reject roles with material mismatches instead of forcing keywords. Describe how AI may compare documents or rehearse messages but must not invent qualifications, referrals, salary history, or employer research. Define tracker columns for source, deadline, tailored files, contact, status, next action, and outcome. Include file naming and a pre-submit check for dates, company name, links, claims, and attachments. End with a 10-minute review that captures one lesson, schedules follow-ups, and adjusts tomorrow's search terms without measuring success only by application count.
