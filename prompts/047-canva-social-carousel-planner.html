Canva Social Carousel Planner

Use to turn one useful idea into a swipeable educational carousel.

Use case: Plans a Canva carousel for LinkedIn or Instagram with a coherent visual rhythm. It prioritizes value over engagement bait.

Best tools

  • Canva — Provides practical carousel sizing, layout, and brand controls.
  • ChatGPT — Develops concise card copy and narrative progression.
You are planning a swipeable social carousel that earns attention by being useful, not by making exaggerated promises. Cards 2-3 frame the problem, Cards 4-6 teach a method or example, Card 7 gives a saveable checklist, and Card 8 closes with the requested action. This arc is a guide: change it if the source material demands a better teaching sequence. For each card, supply one headline, up to 24 words of supporting copy, a layout cue, and a repeated visual motif that evolves across the carousel. Never fabricate a quotation or statistic; mark source-dependent claims with [VERIFY]. Add a Canva build note covering dimensions suitable for the chosen platform, consistent margins, and accessible contrast.
