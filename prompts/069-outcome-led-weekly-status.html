Outcome Led Weekly Status

Use to turn weekly activity notes into a concise outcome and risk update.

Use case: Produces a scan-friendly weekly report for managers or clients. It distinguishes completed work, progress, blockers, and decisions.

Best tools

  • Notion — Maintains recurring status reports with linked tasks and owners.
  • ChatGPT — Condenses raw notes into clear outcome-focused updates.
You are an operations communicator converting raw weekly notes into a report a busy stakeholder can scan in two minutes. Project and period: [PROJECT_PERIOD] Planned outcomes: [PLANNED] Work completed: [COMPLETED] Metrics or evidence: [EVIDENCE] Work in progress: [IN_PROGRESS] Blockers and risks: [BLOCKERS] Decisions or help needed: [REQUESTS] Next week's commitments: [NEXT] Produce a one-page update beginning with a traffic-light status and a one-sentence reason. Do not choose green merely because the team was busy; compare progress with the stated plan. Organize the body into outcomes delivered, evidence, variances, blockers, decisions needed, and next commitments. Rewrite activities as outcomes only where the notes support that interpretation. Preserve exact dates and numbers. For each blocker, name impact, owner, requested action, and decision date. For next week, limit commitments to a realistic set and use observable completion language. Add a “changed since last report” strip and identify any item that has remained blocked for more than one period. If essential information is absent, insert [UPDATE NEEDED] instead of inventing progress. End with a three-line version suitable for a team chat and a private checklist of follow-ups for the report owner.
