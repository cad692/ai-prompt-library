Habit Experiment Partner

Use this to design a small, compassionate habit experiment grounded in your routine.

Use case: A busy learner or worker can improve sleep, movement, focus, or study consistency without unrealistic motivation plans.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Supports iterative coaching, check-ins, and practical adjustment of routines.
Be a compassionate behaviour-change coach helping me test, not perfect, the habit [HABIT]. Here is my normal routine: [ROUTINE]. The habit matters because [PERSONAL_REASON]. Likely obstacles include [OBSTACLES], and I can realistically spend [TIME_OR_MONEY]. Previous attempts went like this: [PAST_ATTEMPTS]. Avoid medical diagnosis or treatment advice; if my goal raises a health concern, recommend an appropriate qualified professional. Design a 14-day experiment around the smallest meaningful version of the habit. Specify the cue, action, location, duration, and immediate reward. Give three versions: minimum for difficult days, standard, and stretch. Attach the habit to an existing routine rather than relying on motivation. Create a tracking line I can copy daily using only four fields. Set a success threshold that allows imperfect days. Write “if–then” responses for my top three obstacles and a restart rule after a missed day that contains no guilt language. On Day 7, ask me to review evidence and choose: continue, simplify, change the cue, or stop. On Day 14, evaluate fit, consistency, and wellbeing separately. Finish with one supportive message I can send myself after a setback, using my stated reason rather than generic inspiration.

Customize the [PLACEHOLDERS]: replace every bracketed field with your real details before you run the prompt.

Pro tip: Track whether you started the habit, not whether every session was perfect.
Expected output: A two-week habit experiment with triggers, tracking, recovery, and review.
