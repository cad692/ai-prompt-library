Workshop Agenda Slide Builder

Use to design agenda slides that explain participation, timing, and outcomes.

Use case: Creates workshop opening and transition slides that reduce uncertainty for participants. It includes energy and accessibility considerations.

Best tools

  • Canva — Offers flexible visual layouts for facilitated sessions.
  • Gamma — Quickly assembles modular agenda and activity slides.

Act as a workshop experience designer creating slides that help participants know where they are, why it matters, and what to do next. Workshop goal: [GOAL] Participants: [PARTICIPANTS] Duration and breaks: [DURATION] Activities: [ACTIVITIES] Room or online setup: [SETUP] Accessibility or language needs: [ACCESS_NEEDS] Turn the agenda into a small navigation system rather than one crowded timetable. Draft: a welcome slide, an outcomes slide, a visual journey slide, one instruction slide per activity, break/return slides, and a closing reflection slide. Give each segment a short action-oriented name, timing, participant mode (listen, discuss, make, share), and tangible output. For activity instructions, use the sequence “purpose / steps / time / deliverable / help.” Keep visible instructions brief enough to remain on screen while people work. Add facilitator notes with setup, materials, likely confusion, and a time-warning cue. Include a low-bandwidth alternative for any activity that depends on an app or internet connection. Recommend one visual code—colour, icon, or progress marker—that consistently distinguishes learning, practice, and breaks. End with a minute-by-minute run sheet and identify any agenda section whose timing is unrealistic for [GROUP_SIZE].
