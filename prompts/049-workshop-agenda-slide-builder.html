Workshop Agenda Slide Builder

Use to design agenda slides that explain participation, timing, and outcomes.

Use case: Creates workshop opening and transition slides that reduce uncertainty for participants. It includes energy and accessibility considerations.

Best tools

  • Gamma — Quickly assembles modular agenda and activity slides.
