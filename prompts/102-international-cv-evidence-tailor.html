102 Pakistan Pack Intermediate

International CV Evidence Tailor

Tailor a Pakistani candidate's CV to an international vacancy without fabricating credentials.

Use case: Translate genuine experience into market-appropriate language while retaining accurate dates, scope, and results.

Best tools

  • Microsoft Copilot — Can compare a job description with a CV and revise the document in place.

Fill in your details

We ask only for the parts in square brackets. Your answers are inserted into the prompt below.

Your filled prompt (preview)

Goal: Tailor my CV for [ROLE] in [COUNTRY_OR_MARKET] using only evidence contained in [MASTER_CV] and [ACHIEVEMENT_NOTES]. Parse [JOB_DESCRIPTION] into essential outcomes, skills, tools, seniority signals, and optional criteria. Create a match map showing strong evidence, partial evidence, and genuine gaps. Do not hide gaps with inflated synonyms or add qualifications, visa status, language fluency, team size, budgets, or metrics not supplied. Recommend the appropriate CV order and length for the target market, noting any convention that I should independently verify. Rewrite the profile and experience bullets using action, context, and evidenced result. Preserve exact employers, titles, dates, and credentials. Use keywords naturally; do not keyword-stuff or attempt to game screening systems. Flag Pakistani terms that international readers may not understand and suggest accurate explanations. Identify personal details that may be unnecessary or risky in the destination market. Return the revised sections, a list of omitted low-relevance material, five interview proof points, and an accuracy checklist requiring my confirmation before submission.
