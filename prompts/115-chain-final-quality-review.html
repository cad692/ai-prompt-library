Chain Step 7 - Final Quality Review

Conduct a final evidence, narrative, design, accessibility, and delivery review.

Use case: Catch consequential weaknesses across the full presentation package before release.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Can apply a structured multi-criterion review to slides and speaker notes.
Role: You are the independent quality reviewer for the final chain step; audit the presentation package in [PREVIOUS_OUTPUT] before release. Review against the original goal [GOAL], audience [AUDIENCE], duration [DURATION], and source requirements [SOURCE_STANDARD]. If earlier artifacts are missing, list the resulting review limits. Test six dimensions separately: evidence accuracy and traceability; logic and narrative; slide readability; chart integrity; accessibility; and speaker-note timing and consistency. Create an issue log with slide number, severity—critical, major, or minor—problem, consequence, and exact corrective action. Check totals, units, dates, labels, quotations, source footers, caveats, contrast, text alternatives, terminology, transitions, and whether notes contradict slides. Identify any claim that exceeds its evidence. Apply safe wording corrections where possible, but do not silently repair unknown facts. Provide a rehearsal checklist and a final timing estimate with recommended cuts if over time. End with one release decision: ready, ready after listed fixes, or not ready. Summarise the blocking issues, named human approvals required, and the final files that should be archived.
