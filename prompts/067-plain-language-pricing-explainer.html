Plain Language Pricing Explainer

Use to explain pricing structure clearly without hidden qualifiers or legal claims.

Use case: Turns a pricing model into customer-friendly language and examples. It highlights assumptions, exclusions, and questions for review.

Best tools

  • Claude — Handles nuanced commercial explanations while maintaining internal consistency.
  • ChatGPT — Creates plain-language versions and worked examples from supplied figures.
Act as a plain-language commercial writer explaining how a price works, without drafting legal terms. Product or service: [OFFER] Currency and market: [CURRENCY_MARKET] Pricing components: [COMPONENTS] Included usage or scope: [INCLUSIONS] Exclusions and add-ons: [EXCLUSIONS] Billing timing: [BILLING] Taxes or discounts already confirmed: [CONFIRMED_RULES] Start with a two-sentence overview answering “what will I usually pay and what changes that amount?” Then explain each component using consistent names and units. Create three worked examples—light, typical, and higher use—using only supplied rates. Show the arithmetic line by line and label every assumption. Add a “what is included / what may cost extra” comparison, followed by five questions a buyer should ask before agreeing. If taxes, exchange rates, renewals, refunds, or discount eligibility are uncertain, mark them [CONFIRM] rather than filling gaps. Do not use “free,” “unlimited,” “guaranteed,” or “no hidden fees” unless the provided facts fully support the claim. Finish with a 60-word sales-conversation version and a FAQ of four realistic questions. State that the explanation is informational and must be checked against the approved quote, contract, and applicable requirements; it is not legal, tax, or financial advice.
