Source-to-Decision Brief Workflow

Plan a traceable path from research question to concise decision brief.

Use case: Coordinate search, evidence capture, synthesis, writing, and review while preserving source provenance.

Best tools

  • Perplexity — Helps discover web sources and provides links that can be independently checked.

Assign discovery to [SEARCH_TOOL], source capture to [NOTES_TOOL], comparison to [ANALYSIS_TOOL], and drafting to [WRITING_TOOL].
