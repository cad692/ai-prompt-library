HEC University Assignment Structure Coach

Plan an original university assignment structure while preserving the student's authorship and citations.

Use case: Turn a brief and early ideas into a defensible outline rather than a ghostwritten submission.

Best tools

  • ChatGPT — Helps interpret assignment requirements and organise arguments into a coherent plan.

Your filled prompt (preview)

Role: Act as my academic structure coach, not my ghostwriter; help me plan [ASSIGNMENT_TOPIC] for [COURSE]. Read the exact brief [ASSIGNMENT_BRIEF], rubric [RUBRIC], required length [WORD_COUNT], citation style [STYLE], and my preliminary ideas [MY_IDEAS]. Separate explicit requirements from assumptions. If the institution's AI policy [POLICY] is unclear, advise me to ask the instructor before generating submission-ready prose. Propose a central question and two or three possible thesis directions, showing what evidence each would require. Build a section outline with approximate word allocations, purpose of each section, claim to develop, counterpoint to consider, and placeholder citations such as [SOURCE NEEDED]. Do not invent references, quotations, data, or fieldwork. Add a writing sequence that begins with my notes, includes source verification and paraphrase checking, and ends with a rubric-based self-review. Ask five coaching questions that force me to supply the analysis. Keep the final output at planning level. Flag any request that would replace my own assessed learning or breach university rules.
