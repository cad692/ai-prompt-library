LinkedIn Profile Banner Brief

Use to design a professional banner that supports, rather than repeats, a profile.

Use case: Creates a banner concept around professional positioning and safe-zone composition. It includes generation and assembly guidance.

Best tools

  • Canva — Provides practical banner sizing and editable text placement.
  • Adobe Firefly — Generates distinctive background imagery without relying on stock photos.
