## Exchange & Pakistan access (general)

- Most international AI tools bill in **USD** on Visa/Mastercard. Many Pakistani users face declined cards, 3-D Secure friction, or need virtual USD cards / Wise / Payoneer.
- Prefer tools with: free tiers, local payment (where available), or Google/Microsoft billing already used in Pakistan.
- **Approx PKR** below = USD × 278, rounded to nearest 50 for readability. Recheck before printing final student packs.

## Assistants

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- **Best for:** General writing, brainstorming, coding help, image gen (paid), file analysis, custom GPTs.
- **Free:** Unlimited everyday text chats (subject to abuse limits); limited uploads, images, voice, deep research, memory. Ads may appear on Free/Go in some markets.
- **Paid (US list):** Go **$8/mo**; Plus **$20/mo**; Pro **$200/mo**. Business/Enterprise separate. Sources: openai.com / chatgpt.com pricing (2026); OpenAI “ChatGPT Go worldwide” announcement.
- **Approx PKR/mo:** Go ~2,200; Plus ~5,550; Pro ~55,600.
- **Limits:** Rate limits by tier; model names change often.
- **Privacy:** Consumer chats may be used to improve models unless opted out (check current OpenAI settings). Do not paste secrets/PII.
- **Pakistan:** Generally available; payment usually USD card. Go is the budget paid tier for students.

### Gemini (Google)
- **Best for:** Google ecosystem (Gmail/Docs/Drive), multimodal, Deep Research, NotebookLM companion, long context.
- **Free:** Gemini app free tier with usage caps.
- **Paid:** Google AI plans (Plus / Pro / Ultra). **Dollar amounts on one.google.com were not fully rendered in our fetch** (page hid prices). Secondary sources (2026): Plus ~**$4.99/mo**, Pro ~**$19.99/mo**, Ultra **$99.99** / **$199.99**. Mark Plus/Pro exact sticker as **confirm at checkout**. Student: “Google AI Pro free for 1 year” offer — **availability varies by country**; verify Pakistan eligibility.
- **Approx PKR/mo (if secondary correct):** Plus ~1,400; Pro ~5,550; Ultra 5x ~27,800.
- **Privacy:** Google account policies; Workspace/enterprise differ.
- **Pakistan:** Gemini free widely used; paid Google One AI availability — verify country list.

### Claude (Anthropic)
- **Best for:** Long-form writing, careful analysis, coding (Claude Code on paid), documents/artifacts.
- **Free:** Chat, web search, artifacts, limited projects (up to 5), usage caps on 5-hour windows.
- **Paid:** Pro **$20/mo** or **$17/mo** annual ($200/yr); Max from **$100/mo** (5x/20x); Team Standard **$25/mo** or **$20** annual; Premium seats **$125/mo** or **$100** annual. Source: claude.com/pricing (fetched 3 Oct 2026).
- **Approx PKR/mo:** Pro ~5,550; Max from ~27,800.
- **Privacy:** Consumer opt-out of training; Team/Enterprise no training by default.
- **Pakistan:** Available where Anthropic supports; USD card typical.

### Microsoft Copilot
- **Best for:** Users already in Word/Excel/PowerPoint/Outlook; Windows/Edge.
- **Free:** Copilot Chat (web-grounded) with Microsoft account; limited vs licensed Copilot in apps.
- **Paid (individuals):** Copilot bundled into Microsoft 365 Personal **$9.99/mo**, Family **$12.99/mo**, Premium **$19.99/mo** (US). Business Copilot add-on historically ~**$21–30**/user/mo depending on SKU; regional pages vary. Source: microsoft.com Copilot pricing pages.
- **Approx PKR/mo:** Personal ~2,800; Premium ~5,550.
- **Pakistan:** Microsoft 365 commonly sold; confirm local Copilot feature parity.

### Grok (xAI)
- **Best for:** Real-time X/web flavour, casual Q&A, image/video on higher tiers.
- **Free:** Generous free tier on grok.com / X.
- **Paid:** SuperGrok **$30/mo**; SuperGrok Plus **$100/mo**; Lite/Heavy also listed on some comparisons. Source: x.ai/pricing (fetched 3 Oct 2026). X Premium/Premium+ may bundle higher limits.
- **Approx PKR/mo:** SuperGrok ~8,350; Plus ~27,800.
- **Pakistan:** Accessible via web/X where not blocked; payment friction same as USD apps.

### DeepSeek
- **Best for:** Strong reasoning/coding on a free chat; cheap API for builders.
- **Free:** Consumer chat (web/app) free; may throttle under load. No consumer “Plus” subscription as of mid/late 2026 sources.
- **Paid:** API only — e.g. deepseek-flash / deepseek-v4-pro per 1M tokens (peak/off-peak). Source: api-docs.deepseek.com (fetched).
- **Privacy:** China-based provider; avoid sensitive personal/client data. Data residency concerns for regulated work.
- **Pakistan:** Chat often works; popular with students for cost.

### Meta AI
- **Best for:** Quick help inside WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook; casual generation.
- **Free:** Consumer Meta AI free (no paid consumer tier found).
- **Paid:** N/A for consumers. Hosted API changes — treat enterprise API status as **verify**.
- **Privacy:** Meta platform data practices; not for confidential work.
- **Pakistan:** High relevance via WhatsApp.

## Research & search

### Perplexity
- **Free:** Practically unlimited basic search; ~**3 Pro Searches/day**; limited Research. Source: Perplexity Help Center (modified Sep 2026).
- **Pro:** ~**$20/mo** or **$17/mo** annual. Max ~**$200/mo** / **$167** annual. Education Pro discounts via SheerID (~$10/mo mentioned).
- **Best for:** Cited web answers, quick fact-finding.
- **Caveat:** Always open citations; AI can mis-cite.

### NotebookLM (Google)
- **Best for:** Studying your own PDFs/notes; audio overviews; source-grounded Q&A.
- **Free:** Strong free tier. Higher limits via Google AI plans.
- **Pakistan:** Excellent for students with uploaded course packs (respect copyright).

### Consensus / Elicit
- **Consensus:** Free + Pro ~**$20/mo** or **$144/yr** (secondary, 2026). Peer-reviewed answer focus.
- **Elicit:** Free basic; Plus ~**$12/mo**; Pro ~**$49/mo** annual billing (secondary). Literature review / extraction.
- Mark exact entitlements as **confirm on site** before workshop print.

### Google AI Mode / AI Overviews in Search
- Built into Search where rolled out; not a separate subscription. Feature availability varies by country/language.

- **ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini file upload:** Built-in analysis on free (limited) and paid.

## Presentations & design

### Gamma
- Free plan (credits; export options). Plus ~**$9**/seat/mo annual; Pro ~**$18**; Ultra ~**$90**. Source: gamma.app/pricing + secondary comparisons (2026). Exact credit counts change — confirm.

### Canva AI (Magic Studio)
- Free tier with limited AI uses. Pro commonly ~**$15–18/mo** region-dependent. Strong for social graphics + Pakistan-friendly templates.

### Beautiful.ai
- Trial (card often required). Pro ~**$12–14.50/mo** annual depending on source date; Teams ~**$40**/user. Source: beautiful.ai/pricing.

### Copilot in PowerPoint
- Requires eligible Microsoft 365 + Copilot entitlement.

## Productivity / data / add-ins / coding

- **Excel/Sheets AI:** Copilot in Excel (M365); Gemini in Sheets (Workspace/Google AI). Feature flags vary.
- **Notion AI:** Bundled mainly on Business (~$20/user/mo annual). Free/Plus have limited AI trial.
- **Otter / Fireflies:** Free tiers for meeting notes; paid for longer retention — **confirm current minutes**.
- **Zapier / Make:** Zapier Free ~100 tasks/mo; Professional from ~$20–30/mo. Make has competitive free/paid.
- **Grammarly:** Free basics; Pro ~$12/mo annual (secondary).
- **Browser extensions:** Official Copilot/Gemini sidebars preferred over random “AI writers.”
- **Cursor:** Hobby free; Pro **$20/mo**; Pro Plus **$60**; Ultra **$200**. India **Start ₹649/mo** (Cursor Models pool only). Source: cursor.com/docs (fetched).
- **GitHub Copilot:** Free limited; Pro ~**$10/mo**; Business ~**$19**/user (secondary 2026 — confirm github.com/features/copilot).
- **Replit:** Free + Core paid — **confirm** current Core price before print.

## Vendor-neutral recommendations (evidence-based)

| Need | Free-first pick | Upgrade when… |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Everyday Q&A | ChatGPT Free / Gemini Free / DeepSeek / Meta AI (WhatsApp) | Hit daily limits or need deep research |
